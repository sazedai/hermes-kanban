# Marketing Pipeline — Momentum Launch

## Task 1

**Title:** Audience Research — who is the Momentum user?
**Assignee:** researcher
**Depends On:** None
**Description:** Research the target user for Momentum, an AI habit tracker that adapts to natural energy patterns. Find: demographics, primary pain points (what are they Googling?), preferred platforms (Reddit, TikTok, LinkedIn?), behavioral patterns around habit tracking. Output: a structured brief with personas, top 3 pain points, and platform breakdown.
**Output:** Structured research brief
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Minimum 5 distinct sources
- At least 2 defined personas
- Top 3 pain points with evidence
- Platform breakdown with audience size estimates
- Confidence rating (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)

## Task 2

**Title:** Messaging & Positioning — what's our angle?
**Assignee:** analyst
**Depends On:** Task 1
**Description:** Based on the audience research from T1, define the core messaging for Momentum. What problem do we own? What's our one-liner? How are we different from static habit apps like Habitica, Streaks, or Todoist? Output: a positioning doc with problem statement, target persona quote, and 3 key differentiators.
**Output:** Messaging framework document
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Clear problem statement (one sentence)
- Target persona quote (in user's voice)
- Full positioning statement
- 3 specific, defensible differentiators
- One-liner under 20 words
- Tone & voice guidelines

## Task 3

**Title:** Landing Page Copy — hero, features, CTA
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 2
**Description:** Write landing page copy for Momentum. Based on the messaging framework from T2. Include: hero headline + subheadline, 3 feature highlights, one-liner pitch, social proof placeholder (tagline only), and primary CTA. Tone: warm, encouraging, science-backed. Max 500 words total.
**Output:** Landing page copy
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Hero headline ≤ 10 words
- Subheadline ≤ 20 words
- 3 feature bullets ≤ 15 words each
- Total ≤ 500 words
- Uses problem statement from T2
- CTA is clear and singular

## Task 4

**Title:** Email Launch Sequence — 3 emails
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 2
**Description:** Write a 3-email launch sequence for Momentum. Based on the messaging framework from T2. Email 1: The Hook — introduce the problem (rigid habit apps don't adapt). Email 2: The Demo — show how Momentum works in one day. Email 3: The Close — streak starter offer. Each email: subject line + body, max 150 words each. Tone: conversational, direct, no fluff.
**Output:** 3-email sequence
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Each email has subject + body
- Each body ≤ 150 words
- Email 1 is problem-first
- Email 2 is solution-first
- Email 3 has urgency + CTA
- All use messaging from T2

## Task 5

**Title:** Social Posts — LinkedIn, X, Instagram
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 2
**Description:** Write 6 social posts for the Momentum launch — 2 per platform. Platforms: LinkedIn (professional, thought-leadership tone), X/Twitter (punchy, hook-first, <280 chars), Instagram (visual-first captions, casual, emoji-friendly). Based on the messaging framework from T2. Topics: introduce Momentum, contrast with rigid habit apps, show the energy-adaptive angle.
**Output:** 6 social media posts
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 2 LinkedIn posts (professional, insight-first)
- 2 X/Twitter posts (<280 chars, hook-first)
- 2 Instagram posts (casual, emoji-friendly)
- All reference messaging from T2
- Each post works standalone

## Task 6

**Title:** Blog Post — SEO-optimized, 1500 words
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 2
**Description:** Write a 1500-word blog post for Momentum. SEO-targeted toward: 'best habit tracker app 2026' and 'AI habit tracker'. Based on the messaging framework from T2. Structure: Hook (problem with current habit apps), What is Momentum (energy-adaptive scheduling), How it works (simple 3-step explanation), Why it beats rigid apps (comparison table), CTA (start free trial). Include 3 subheadings, one CTA block.
**Output:** Blog post (1500 words)
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- 1500 words ±50
- SEO keywords included naturally
- 3 H2 subheadings
- 1 comparison table
- 1 CTA block
- Uses messaging from T2

## Task 7

**Title:** YouTube Script — launch video for Momentum
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 2
**Description:** Write a 60-second YouTube script for a Momentum launch video. Based on the messaging framework from T2. Structure: Hook (3 seconds, problem-first), What is Momentum (10 seconds, demo the concept), How it works (20 seconds, 3-step), Why it adapts (15 seconds, the insight), CTA (12 seconds, subscribe + link in bio). Include on-camera cues, approximate timings, and voiceover text. Tone: energetic, relatable, confident.
**Output:** YouTube Shorts script
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Total runtime ≈ 60 seconds
- Timing cues for each section
- [ON CAMERA] / [VOICEOVER] labels
- Uses messaging from T2
- Energetic, relatable tone

## Task 8

**Title:** Review — consistency and accuracy check across all outputs
**Assignee:** reviewer
**Depends On:** Task 3, Task 4, Task 5, Task 6, Task 7
**Description:** Review all 5 content outputs (T3: landing page, T4: email sequence, T5: social posts, T6: blog post, T7: YouTube script) for brand consistency. Check: (1) Are all 5 using the same problem statement from the messaging framework? (2) Is the tone consistent across all formats? (3) Are there any factual contradictions between pieces? Output: a structured review note with: PASS or REQUEST REVISIONS, list of specific issues found, and suggested fixes for each.
**Output:** Review report
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All 5 pieces reviewed
- Each piece scored PASS/REVISION NEEDED
- Specific issues with specific fixes
- Cross-piece consistency checked
- Priority-ordered fix list

## Task 9

**Title:** Finalize — incorporate review feedback
**Assignee:** writer
**Depends On:** Task 8
**Description:** Incorporate all feedback from the review in T8. Revise the flagged outputs and lock the final versions. Output: the finalized versions of all 5 content pieces, with a change log noting what was fixed from the review.
**Output:** Finalized content package
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All blocking issues from T8 addressed
- Change log documenting what was fixed
- Final versions of all 5 pieces
- Consistent messaging across all pieces
