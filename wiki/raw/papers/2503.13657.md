# Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?

**Authors**: Mert Cemri, Melissa Z. Pan, Shuyi Yang, Lakshya A. Agrawal, Bhavya Chopra, Rishabh Tiwari, Kurt Keutzer, Aditya Parameswaran, Dan Klein, Kannan Ramchandran, Matei Zaharia, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Ion Stoica
**Published**: March 17, 2025
**arXiv ID**: 2503.13657
**Subject**: cs.AI

## Abstract
Despite enthusiasm for Multi-Agent LLM Systems (MAS), their performance gains on popular benchmarks are often minimal. This paper presents the first comprehensive study of MAS challenges.

## Key Contributions
1. **MAST-Data** — 1,600+ annotated traces from 7 popular MAS frameworks
2. **MAST Taxonomy** — First formal taxonomy for classifying MAS failures (14 modes in 3 clusters)
3. **LLM-as-a-Judge Pipeline** — Scalable annotation method with high human agreement

## Failure Categories
- System design issues
- Inter-agent misalignment
- Task verification failures

## Relevance
Essential reading for anyone building multi-agent systems. Provides diagnostic framework for understanding why sub-agent coordination fails.
