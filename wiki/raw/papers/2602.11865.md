# Intelligent AI Delegation

**Authors**: Nenad Tomašev, Matija Franklin, Simon Osindero
**Published**: February 12, 2026
**arXiv ID**: 2602.11865
**Subject**: cs.AI

## Abstract
AI agents need to meaningfully decompose problems into manageable sub-components, and safely delegate their completion across to other AI agents and humans alike. Existing methods rely on simple heuristics and cannot dynamically adapt to environmental changes.

## Key Contributions
Adaptive framework for intelligent AI delegation addressing:
1. Task Allocation — Which tasks to delegate and to whom
2. Transfer of Authority — Granting decision-making power
3. Transfer of Responsibility — Assigning ownership of outcomes
4. Accountability — Mechanisms for answerability
5. Clear Role Specifications — Defining roles and boundaries
6. Clarity of Intent — Well-communicated goals
7. Trust Mechanisms — Building trust between parties

## Relevance
Directly addresses the delegation problem in multi-agent systems. Applicable to both human and AI delegators in complex delegation networks.
