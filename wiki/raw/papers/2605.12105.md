# Autonomy and Agency in Agentic AI: Architectural Tactics for Regulated Contexts

**Authors**: Damir Safin, Dian Balta
**Published**: May 12, 2026
**arXiv ID**: 2605.12105
**Subject**: cs.AI

## Abstract
Deploying agentic AI in regulated environments demands careful balancing of two interdependent design dimensions: **Agency** (what the system can do) and **Autonomy** (how much it acts without human involvement).

## Key Contributions
1. **Two-Dimensional Design Space** — Five operational levels each for autonomy (L1 human-commanded → L5 fully autonomous) and agency (L1 reasoning over context → L5 committed writes)
2. **Six Architectural Tactics** — Checkpoints, Escalation, Multi-agent delegation, Tool provisioning, Tool fencing, Write staging
3. **Deployment Parameters** — Model capability, agent architecture, tool fidelity, workflow bottlenecks, evaluation methods

## Relevance to Sub-Agent Delegation
This paper directly addresses the design of multi-agent systems where sub-agents operate with bounded authority. The "multi-agent delegation" tactic is most relevant — distributing tasks among specialized agents with bounded authority.
