## 3 Feature Highlights

**1. Energy-Adaptive Scheduling**
On low-energy days, Momentum shows you the minimum viable version. On high-energy days, you go all in. No more binary pass/fail.

**2. Keystone Focus, Not Habit Hoarding**
Momentum recommends 2-3 keystone habits at a time and protects your focus -- because tracking 15 things means finishing none of them.

**3. Pattern Insights That Explain Your Behavior**
Discover cause-and-effect patterns like "You're 80% more likely to exercise on days you sleep 7+ hours" -- so you know what actually moves the needle.
