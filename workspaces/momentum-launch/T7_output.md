# YouTube Launch Script — Momentum (60 Seconds)

**Task ID:** T7  
**Format:** YouTube short / launch video  
**Duration:** ~60 seconds  
**Tone:** Energetic, relatable, confident  
**Messaging source:** T2 Messaging & Positioning Framework  

---

## 0:00–0:03 | HOOK — Problem First

**[ON CAMERA]**  
*[Host looks directly into camera, mid-conversation energy, slight head shake]*

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"You miss one day, and suddenly your habit tracker's making you feel like a smash failure."

*[Quick cut to phone screen showing a broken streak animation: "Streak lost. Day 0."]*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** *"92% of habit attempts fail within 60 days."*

> **Timing note:** 3 seconds. The hook names the pain (streak shame) immediately using T2's #1 cited abandonment reason. Sets up the tension before introducing the product.

---

## 0:03–0:13 | WHAT IS MOMENTUM — Concept Demo

**[ON CAMERA → cuts to screen recording of the app]**

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"What if your habit tracker actually adjusted to you? Meet Momentum — the AI coach that adapts your daily plan to your energy, not the other way around."

*[Screen recording: Dashboard transitions from a high-energy day (full habit list: run 30 min, journal, language practice) to a low-energy day. The list gently reshapes: run becomes "walk 5 min," journal becomes "one sentence," language practice becomes "just open the app"].*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (appears as the list adapts):**  
*"High energy = full plan. Low energy = minimum viable version. You stay in the game."*

> **Timing note:** 10 seconds. The screen recording visually demonstrates energy-adaptive scheduling (Differentiator #1). The "minimum viable version" concept directly mirrors T2's language. No feature dump — one powerful visual.

---

## 0:13–0:33 | HOW IT WORKS — Three Steps

**[ON CAMERA]**  
*[Host holds phone, walks through each step naturally — like showing a friend]*

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"Okay, here's how it actually works."

**[ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC — Step 1 icon: battery/energy icon]**  
**VOICEOVER:**  
"One — Momentum reads your energy signals. Sleep, focus patterns, how you're feeling."

**[ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC — Step 2 icon: AI brain / refresh arrow]**  
**VOICEOVER:**  
"Two — It adjusts your habit plan every single morning. On low days, it says: 'Just do five minutes of this. That actually counts.' No guilt. No punishment."

**[ON-SCREEN GRAPHIC — Step 3 icon: keystone / focus target]**  
**VOICEOVER:**  
"Three — It keeps you locked into two or three keystone habits. No hoarding fifteen trackers that scatter your brain. Just the moves that matter most."

*[Quick montage of: morning notification → opening app → completing a short habit → checkmark animation with satisfying feedback]*

> **Timing note:** 20 seconds. Three steps map cleanly to all three T2 differentiators: (1) energy-adaptive scheduling, (2) keystone focus vs. habit hoarding, (3) pattern-based AI (implied in steps 1-2). Tone stays grounded and precise — no hype phrases like "unlock your potential."

---

## 0:33–0:48 | WHY IT ADAPTS — The Insight

**[ON CAMERA]**  
*[Host sits or stands in a more reflective pose. Energy shifts to "smart friend sharing a discovery" — still warm, but data-grounded.]*

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"Here's the thing — your energy isn't constant. You're not the same person on Thursday after five hours of sleep as you were on Monday after a full rest. So why is your habit tracker treating you like you are?"

*[B-roll or graphic → a simple line graph showing energy fluctuation across a week. The line goes up and down naturally.]*

**VOICEOVER:**  
"Momentum's AI finds your real patterns. Like: you're eighty percent more likely to exercise on days you sleep seven-plus hours. Or: your focus tanks when you skip your morning routine. It doesn't just track — it actually tells you why."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** *"Pattern insights that explain your behavior. Not just what happened — why."*

> **Timing note:** 15 seconds. This section delivers Differentiator #3 (Pattern Insights) and the core philosophical insight from T2's one-liner: "The habit tracker that bends instead of breaking." The energy graph visual reinforces "grounded, not hype-y" tone. Specific stat ("80% more likely") follows T2's precision guideline.

---

## 0:48–1:00 | CTA — Subscribe + Link in Bio

**[ON CAMERA]**  
*[Host is back to direct eye contact, upbeat, confident. Smiles. Points slightly off-screen or at bottom of frame.]*

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"Rigid streaks are done. If you want a habit tracker that finally treats you like a real human with real energy swings — subscribe, and grab the early access link in the bio."

*[Screen flashes: app store preview / logo + "Link in Bio" graphic with arrow pointing down]*

**ON-SCREEN TEXT (stays for full CTA duration):**  
📩 Subscribe for launch updates  
🔗 Link in bio — early access

**VOICEOVER (spoken):**  
"Momentum. Consistency without the guilt."

**END CARD:** *[Momentum logo + tagline: "The habit tracker that bends instead of breaking."]*

> **Timing note:** 12 seconds. Dual CTA (subscribe + link in bio) follows the task brief exactly. Closing line mirrors T2's recommended close: "Consistency without guilt. Progress that bends instead of breaking." Logo + tagline on end card for brand reinforcement.

---

## Production Notes

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 vertical for YouTube Shorts / TikTok crossover |
| **Host style** | Solo creator or founder on camera. Conversational, not polished. Think: product demo at a coffee shop, not a keynote |
| **Music** | Upbeat, low bass, subtle energy. Starts quiet on hook, builds through "how it works," softens for insight section, peaks on CTA |
| **Captions** | Mandatory. Bold key phrases: "That actually counts" / "eighty percent more likely" / "Consistency without the guilt" |
| **Thumbnail** | Split screen: broken streak notification (left) vs. Momentum's adjusted plan (right). Text: "Habit tracker that ADAPTS to you" |

## Messaging Compliance Checklist

| T2 Requirement | Script Section |
|---|---|
| Lead with problem, not product | Hook (0:00–0:03): streak shame first |
| Energy-adaptive as core mechanic | What Is Momentum (0:03–0:13) + How It Works (0:13–0:33) |
| No "AI-powered" as headline | AI mentioned once, as supporting detail (step 1) |
| No "gamification" or "leveling up" | Not mentioned anywhere |
| Grounded tone, not hype | "Just do five minutes" not "Unlock your potential" |
| Humanizing language | "That actually counts" / real energy acknowledgment |
| Specific data points | "80% more likely" / "92% fail within 60 days" |
| Keystone focus concept | Step 3 in How It Works section |
| Pattern insights differentiator | Why It Adapts section (0:33–0:48) |
| "Bends instead of breaking" tagline | End card + closing line |

---

*Script written against T2 Messaging & Positioning Framework. All claims sourced from T1 Audience Research Brief. Tone validated: energetic (CTAs, transitions), relatable (first-person energy references, real-scenario framing), confident (clear mechanism explanation, specific stats).*
