## ISSUES SUMMARY

### BLOCKING (must fix before launch)

1. **T4 Email 3 — Streak-centric offer language contradicts T2 positioning.**
   - Subject line: "Start your streak this week (before the offer resets)"
   - Body: "30-day streak starter"
   - CTA: "Start Your Streak"
   - T2 explicitly states: "Streaks as a primary feature. We may show streaks as one data point, but we never make them the core feedback loop."
   - **Suggested fix:** Rename the offer from "30-day streak starter" to "30-day energy-adaptive trial." Change CTA from "Start Your Streak" to "Start Free Trial" (which T3 already uses consistently). Change subject line to something like "Your free trial expires Sunday" or "30 days of guilt-free habit tracking."

2. **T4 vs T6 — Trial duration contradiction.**
   - T4 says 30 days. T6 says 14 days.
   - **Suggested fix:** Confirm the actual trial length with the product team, then update both files to match. If 30 days: change T6 line 93 to "free to try for 30 days." If 14 days: change T4 references from "30-day" to "14-day."

### NON-BLOCKING (fix when convenient)

3. **T6 — "AI habit tracker" used as recurring descriptor rather than supporting detail.**
   - T2 guidance: "Avoid 'AI' as the headline. Lead with the outcome... and use AI as supporting detail."
   - The blog uses phrases like "the AI habit tracker that adapts" and "the AI habit tracker that actually understands" which approach headline usage.
   - **Suggested fix:** Replace occasional instances with "Momentum" or "the app" to reduce AI-branding emphasis. Keep one reference to the AI mechanism in the "How It Works" section.

4. **T6 — "pass-freak mechanics" (line 23) is likely a typo.**
   - **Suggested fix:** Change to "pass/fail mechanics."

5. **T4 Email 2 — "Consistency score: 87%" is a specific metric not established in T2.**
   - **Suggested fix:** Confirm this metric exists in the app. If not, change to a more general statement like "You kept your momentum alive."
